## The joke

> My cat walked across my keyboard mid-call and typed gibberish. My boss replied, 'Best idea you've had all quarter.'

## Why this line

- **Setup and turn:** the cat does something chaotic on camera, and instead of the expected trouble, the boss praises the gibberish. The punchline lands on a reversal, not on a pun.
- **Fresh angle:** no "cats own the house" or "knocked the glass off the table" trope — the joke lives in a work call, a place cats genuinely invade.
- **Tight:** 115 characters, two beats, nothing after the punchline.
- **Retellable:** a one-sentence story anyone can repeat to a friend the same day.

node main.ts
